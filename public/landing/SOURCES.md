# Landing page photographs

The hero and centre photographs are original files from the centres' official websites. The nine specialty cards now use **AI-processed photographic derivatives (V4)** based on source photographs from those websites, requested by the user on 2026-11-09. They must not be described as unmodified documentary photographs. Original files below remain for provenance; the four doctor portraits are no longer displayed.

The complete per-image source-to-output mapping and exact prompts are in `assets/landing/specialties-v4.json`, with a readable index in `docs/landing/IMAGE_SERIES_V4.md`. Full-resolution PNGs are in `assets/landing/processed/v4/`; optimized site files are in `public/landing/specialties/*-v4.jpg`.

Additional input sources: Elisha gallery `2018/08/IMG_0385.jpg` (interventional imaging suite), `2018/08/IMG_0361.jpg` (CT room); Raphael ENT page's `/media/il2cquy2/עיצוב-ללא-שם-38.png` (otoscope examination); Elisha endoscopy page's `2018/05/Endoscopy-11111111.jpg` (endoscope control head). Imaging equipment is illustrative of a clinical environment, not evidence of a particular pain-treatment service or designated pain clinic.
